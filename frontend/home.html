About FixGo AI

FixGo AI is an intelligent home service platform that connects users with trusted professionals including electricians, plumbers, cleaners, carpenters, painters, and many more. The platform uses Artificial Intelligence to analyze household issues, recommend the correct service, and provide simple repair guidance when appropriate. Users can search, compare, book, pay securely, and communicate with workers in one place.
