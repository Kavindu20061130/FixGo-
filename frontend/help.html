Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?
You can sign up using your email or Google account. OTP verification ensures your phone number is valid.
Can I sign in with Google?
Yes, you can use Google Sign-In. This creates a secure account and links your Google profile.
What is OTP verification and why is it needed?
OTP (One‑Time Password) is sent to your phone to confirm your identity and secure your account.
How do I update my profile?
Go to Account Settings (click your avatar) and edit your personal information, including profile picture.
What should I do if I forget my password?
Use the “Forgot Password” link on the login page to reset your password via email OTP.
How do I book a service?
Go to the Services page, select the type of worker you need, choose a date/time, and confirm the booking.
How does the AI Assistant work?
Upload a photo of the problem, and our AI will identify the issue and suggest solutions or recommend a professional.
What is Emergency Mode?
Emergency Mode allows you to request urgent services with priority dispatch, available 24/7 for critical issues.
Can I track my worker in real‑time?
Yes, once a booking is accepted, you can see the worker’s location on a map in real time.
What services does FixGo offer?
We cover electricians, plumbers, carpenters, painters, cleaners, mechanics, AC technicians, CCTV installers, gardeners, and more.
How do I cancel a booking?
You can cancel from the booking details page before the service starts. Cancellation fees may apply.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept all major cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Paytm, and UPI.
How do I get a refund?
Refunds are processed through the payment method used. Contact support with your booking ID.
What is the advance payment policy?
A deposit may be required to secure the booking. The balance is paid after service completion.
Is my payment information secure?
Yes, we use SSL encryption and are PCI DSS compliant. We never store full card details.
How are workers verified?
All workers undergo background checks, identity verification, and skill assessments before joining.
How can I become a worker on FixGo?
Apply through the Worker section. You’ll need to provide documents and pass a verification process.
Can I see worker ratings before booking?
Yes, each worker profile displays ratings and reviews from previous customers.
What if a worker doesn’t show up?
Report the no‑show via the Complaint section; we will investigate and offer a solution.
What if I have a dispute with a worker?
Our dispute resolution team is available to mediate. File a complaint via the Complaints tab.
Is my personal information secure?
Yes, we use SSL encryption and comply with data protection regulations. See our Privacy Policy for details.
What are FixGo’s community guidelines?
Our guidelines promote respectful, honest, and safe interactions. Read them in the Safety & Trust section.
How do I report inappropriate behaviour?
Use the “Report” button on a post or use the Complaint form with evidence.
I’m having trouble with the app. Where can I get help?
Contact support via Live Chat, email, or phone. You can also create a support ticket.
How do I report a bug or technical issue?
Use the Complaint form and select “Technical Bug” as the category. Include screenshots if possible.
How can I provide feedback about the AI Assistant?
You can submit feedback via the Feedback & Review section or directly from the AI Assistant interface.
